Privacy policy

Polyorbit · polyrhythm metronome
Last updated: 16 October 2026

Polyorbit collects no personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

Who is responsible

Polyorbit is made by Josich. Contact: polyorbit.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Polyorbit has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use the microphone, the camera, your contacts or your location. We have no server that receives data from the app.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app saves these on your device only:

  • your settings, wheel, presets, sequence and routines;
  • your practice log, Precision scores, course progress, game levels and awards;
  • the sound-delay calibration of your phone.

This data never leaves your phone unless you send it yourself (see below). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your phone's settings deletes it.

What you choose to share

  • Links (challenges, patterns, routines): the link itself contains the rhythm, its name and, for a challenge, your result. Whoever you send it to can read it. Nothing else is included.
  • Audio, video, score files and challenge cards: made on your phone and handed to the app you pick in your phone's share menu.
  • Backup files: made on your phone; you choose where to keep them (for example Drive or email). We never receive them.

Each of these happens only when you press a button. What happens next is up to the app or service you send them to, under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

If you buy Polyorbit Pro or leave a tip, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Pro, so it can switch Pro on. See Google's privacy policy.

If you write to us

The app's Feedback and survey buttons open your own email app with a message to us; nothing is sent unless you press Send there. We then receive what you write and your email address, and use them only to answer you and to improve Polyorbit. Ask us and we delete your messages.

Network use

The Android app carries all its files inside it: its code and fonts load from nowhere else. The version from Google Play talks only to Google Play, about purchases. The test version downloaded from GitHub also asks GitHub every few hours whether a newer version exists. The web version at josich.github.io/polyrhythm is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address for these requests; we do not receive these records. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

Polyorbit collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data is on your phone and under your control. If you have a question, write to us at the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
